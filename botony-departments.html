PLANT SCIENCE & BIODIVERSITY
DISCOVER OUR DEPARTMENT

Exploring Plant Life, Protecting Our Future

Established in 2016, the Department of Botany provides teaching and laboratory experience in plant biology, ecology, taxonomy, physiology and applied plant science. It connects plant knowledge with agriculture, health, biodiversity, industry and environmental sustainability.

OUR VISION

To advance plant-science education, research and outreach for the responsible use and conservation of biological resources.

OUR MISSION
  • Provide strong theoretical and practical learning in plant science.
  • Develop graduates and researchers who can address agricultural, health and environmental challenges.
  • Promote biodiversity awareness, conservation and sustainable practice.
  • Encourage innovation, research and community outreach.

ACADEMIC PATHWAY

Programmes Offered

The Department of Botany offers undergraduate and postgraduate learning in plant diversity, structure, physiology, genetics, ecology, taxonomy and applied plant science, preparing students for careers in research, agriculture, biotechnology and conservation.

01 UNDERGRADUATE
B.Sc.

B.Sc. Botany

A three-year undergraduate programme studying plant diversity, structure, physiology, genetics, ecology, taxonomy and economic uses. Practical work develops observation, plant identification and laboratory skills.

Learning Focus

  • Plant diversity, taxonomy and anatomy
  • Physiology, genetics and biotechnology basics
  • Ecology, biodiversity and conservation
  • Plant pathology, economic botany and practical field study

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory, herbarium or field assistant
  • Nursery, horticulture and seed-sector roles
  • Environmental and biodiversity projects
  • Agriculture, plantation and botanical-support services
  • Teaching after the required professional qualification
  • Postgraduate study in Botany, biotechnology, ecology or environmental science

02 POSTGRADUATE
M.Sc.

M.Sc. Botany

A postgraduate programme for advanced study of plant science, research methods, ecology, physiology, taxonomy and applied biology. It prepares students to investigate plant systems and contribute to conservation, agriculture and biotechnology.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced plant physiology, taxonomy and genetics
  • Ecology, conservation and environmental biology
  • Plant biotechnology and applied botany
  • Research methods, seminars, fieldwork and project study

Career Opportunities

  • Research assistant or project associate
  • Botanical gardens, herbaria and conservation organizations
  • Agricultural, seed, nursery and biotechnology sectors
  • Environmental consultancy and biodiversity documentation
  • Teaching after meeting eligibility requirements
  • Doctoral research in Botany and allied life sciences
View Syllabus ↗
03 APPLIED LEARNING
Plant

Plant Science & Conservation

Practical learning and research activities help students understand plant identification, biodiversity, ecological relationships and the sustainable use of biological resources.

Learning Opportunities

  • Plant identification and specimen study
  • Herbarium preparation and documentation
  • Biodiversity and ecological observation
  • Fieldwork, laboratory exercises and research projects

Career Opportunities

  • Plant science and field research assistant
  • Biodiversity documentation support
  • Environmental and conservation projects
  • Nursery and horticultural support
  • Botanical and herbarium assistance
  • Further study in plant and environmental sciences

LEARNING THROUGH APPLICATION

Botany Laboratory & Resources

The department reports laboratory instruments, chemicals, specimens, spotters and slides, together with plant and herbarium resources. These support practical learning, plant identification, observation and the study of biodiversity and environmental sustainability.
