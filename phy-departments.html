DISCOVER OUR DEPARTMENT

Exploring the Universe, Inspiring Innovation

Established in 2003, the Department of Physics offers undergraduate and postgraduate learning supported by practical work and faculty guidance. It develops understanding of matter, energy, measurement, instrumentation and the physical principles behind modern technology.

OUR VISION

To motivate women learners to become competent physics professionals and lifelong scientific thinkers.

OUR MISSION
  • Teach physics through clear concepts, experiments and problem-solving.
  • Use innovative methods to connect theory with application.
  • Build laboratory, analytical and research capability.
  • Prepare students for advanced study, education and technology-oriented work.

Programmes Offered

The Department of Physics offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes that combine theoretical understanding, experimental learning and scientific enquiry.

01 UNDERGRADUATE
B.Sc.

B.Sc. Physics

A three-year undergraduate programme covering mechanics, electricity, magnetism, optics, electronics, thermal physics, quantum concepts and practical measurement. It builds scientific reasoning and experimental accuracy.

Learning Focus

  • Mechanics, waves, optics and thermal physics
  • Electricity, magnetism and electronics
  • Modern, atomic and quantum physics
  • Laboratory measurement, instrumentation and computation

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory or instrumentation assistant
  • Electronics, energy, optics and technical-support roles
  • Quality testing and measurement services
  • Teaching after the required professional qualification
  • Scientific and technical competitive examinations
  • M.Sc. Physics, electronics, materials or applied science
View Syllabus ↗
02 POSTGRADUATE
M.Sc.

M.Sc. Physics

A postgraduate programme that advances theoretical, experimental and applied physics. Students strengthen mathematical analysis, instrumentation, research practice and the ability to investigate complex physical systems.

Learning Focus

  • Classical, quantum and statistical physics
  • Electromagnetism, electronics and solid-state physics
  • Instrumentation, computational methods and advanced experiments
  • Research methodology, seminars and project work

Career Opportunities

  • Research assistant or project associate
  • Instrumentation, electronics and testing roles
  • Scientific laboratories and technical organizations
  • Teaching after meeting applicable eligibility norms
  • Data and computational roles with additional skills
  • Ph.D. and advanced research in physics or materials science
View Syllabus ↗
03 PRACTICAL LEARNING
Lab

Physics Laboratory & Research

Practical learning enables students to apply physical principles through experiments, measurements, instrument handling, observations and scientific analysis.

Learning Opportunities

  • Mechanics, optics and thermal physics experiments
  • Electrical and electronic circuit experiments
  • Measurement, calibration and instrumentation
  • Data analysis, computation and scientific reporting
