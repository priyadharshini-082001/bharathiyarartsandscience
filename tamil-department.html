DISCOVER OUR DEPARTMENT

Rooted in Tradition, Inspired by Knowledge

The Department of Tamil nurtures language proficiency, literary appreciation, cultural understanding, communication and research. Since the introduction of B.Lit. Tamil in 2006, the department has expanded through postgraduate and doctoral study, helping students connect classical and modern Tamil scholarship with contemporary communication and professional life.

OUR VISION

To deepen knowledge of Tamil language and literature while preserving cultural values and developing confident, socially responsible women graduates.

OUR MISSION
  • Strengthen reading, writing, speaking, interpretation and research skills.
  • Introduce classical, modern, comparative and applied Tamil studies.
  • Encourage creativity, publication, public speaking and community engagement.
  • Prepare learners for higher education, competitive examinations and language-based careers.

ACADEMIC PATHWAYS

Programmes Offered

Explore opportunities to study Tamil language, literature, cultural traditions and advanced research.

01 UNDERGRADUATE
அ

B.Lit. Tamil

A three-year undergraduate programme providing a foundation in Tamil language, grammar, classical and modern literature, literary criticism, culture and communication.

Learning Focus

  • Tamil grammar, prose, poetry and literary history
  • Classical and modern texts
  • Writing, editing, translation and public speaking
  • Cultural studies and introductory research methods
View Syllabus ↗
02 POSTGRADUATE
இ

M.A. Tamil

An advanced programme for deeper study of Tamil literature, linguistics, criticism, culture and research, developing independent reading and scholarly presentation.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced literary criticism and comparative literature
  • Linguistics, grammar and textual studies
  • Research methodology and academic writing
  • Translation, media communication and cultural documentation
View Syllabus ↗
03 DOCTORAL RESEARCH
ஔ

Ph.D. Tamil

Full-time and part-time research pathways supporting original scholarship in Tamil language, literature, culture and related interdisciplinary fields.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced research design and methodology
  • Textual, linguistic and cultural enquiry
  • Scholarly publication and conference presentation
  • Research ethics, documentation and thesis development

Where Tamil Studies Can Take You

01

Teaching & Academia

02

Writing & Publishing

Content development, copy editing, proofreading and publishing.

03

Translation & Media

04

Research & Heritage

Research projects, doctoral studies, cultural documentation and heritage-related work.
