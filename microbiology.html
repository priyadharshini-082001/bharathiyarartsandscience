Department of Microbiology

About the Department

The Department of Microbiology develops understanding of microorganisms and their importance in health, food, agriculture, environment and industry. The department emphasizes curiosity, critical thinking, laboratory discipline, research competitiveness and practical scientific application.

Students explore the structure, functions, characteristics and interactions of microorganisms through theoretical learning and practical laboratory experience. The programme connects microbiological principles with real-world applications in healthcare, food safety, agriculture, industrial production and environmental management.

Through scientific observation, experimental techniques and research-oriented learning, the department encourages students to develop analytical skills, laboratory accuracy and responsible scientific practices.

Department Highlights

  • Undergraduate programme in B.Sc. Microbiology.
  • Foundations in general, medical and environmental microbiology.
  • Practical training in microbial culture and staining techniques.
  • Microbial applications in food, agriculture and industry.
  • Laboratory discipline, quality practices and biosafety.
  • Research-oriented learning and scientific communication.
  • Career preparation for life-science industries and higher education.

Vision & Mission

Department Vision

To produce qualified and competent microbiology graduates through high-quality education and research-oriented practical training.

Department Mission

  • Provide strong foundations across major areas of microbiology.
  • Develop safe and accurate laboratory skills.
  • Encourage independent thinking, research and scientific communication.
  • Prepare students for life-science industries and advanced education.

B.Sc. Microbiology

About the Course

B.Sc. Microbiology is a three-year undergraduate programme studying microorganisms, their structures, functions, interactions and applications. Students gain knowledge and laboratory experience relevant to medical, food, industrial, agricultural and environmental microbiology.

Learning Focus

  • General, medical and environmental microbiology.
  • Microbial physiology, genetics and immunology.
  • Food, industrial and agricultural microbiology.
  • Microbial culture methods and staining techniques.
  • Sterilization, quality practices and laboratory safety.
  • Scientific observation, practical work and research skills.

Career Opportunities

  • Microbiology laboratory assistant.
  • Food, dairy, water and pharmaceutical quality control.
  • Diagnostic and clinical-laboratory support.
  • Fermentation, production and industrial microbiology roles.
  • Environmental and public-health testing.
  • Postgraduate study in microbiology, biotechnology, public health or allied sciences.

Laboratory Infrastructure

Microbiology Laboratory

The microbiology laboratory supports practical learning in microbial observation, culture techniques, laboratory analysis and scientific investigation. Students develop practical skills while following appropriate laboratory safety and handling procedures.

Laboratory Equipment and Facilities

  • Microscopes for microbial observation.
  • Incubator and shaker for laboratory applications.
  • UV-Vis spectrophotometer for analytical studies.
  • Electrophoresis equipment.
  • Centrifuges for sample processing.
  • Deep freezers for appropriate sample storage.
  • Laminar-air-flow chambers for controlled laboratory work.
  • Molecular-biology laboratory facilities.

Practical Learning Areas

  • Microbial culture and staining techniques.
  • Sterilization and laboratory safety.
  • Microbial physiology and identification fundamentals.
  • Food and environmental microbiology applications.
  • Quality-control methods and laboratory documentation.
  • Introductory molecular-biology techniques.
