About the Department

Mathematics – Since 2003

“Mathematics is the most beautiful and most powerful creation of the human spirit.”

– Stefan Banach

“Mathematics is the Queen of Science and Arithmetic is the Queen of Mathematics.”

– Carl Friedrich Gauss

Our dynamic and energetic Department of Mathematics inspires students to explore the beauty and power of mathematical thinking. Mathematics is the unshaken foundation of science and plays an essential role in developing logical reasoning, analytical ability and problem-solving skills.

The department has been functioning since 2003 for the undergraduate programme and since 2004 for the postgraduate programme. Research activities were introduced in 2012. The department has consistently achieved excellent academic results, producing 100% results in almost every year.

Academic Excellence

Students are encouraged to participate in seminars, conferences, workshops and other academic activities conducted at various institutions. These opportunities help them improve their mathematical knowledge, presentation skills, confidence and exposure to recent developments in the subject.

Faculty Excellence and Research

The department has over 10 faculty members who actively participate in training and professional development programmes. Faculty members contribute to academic advancement through research articles published in national and international journals and by presenting papers at national and international conferences.

University Achievements

The Department of Mathematics takes pride in its academic achievements, having produced 5 Gold Medals and 53 University Ranks. These accomplishments reflect the dedication of the faculty members and the hard work and commitment of the students.

Department Vision

Our Vision

To equip individuals with the ability to think mathematically, reason logically and solve problems effectively.

Our Commitment

The department strives to create a strong mathematical foundation among students by encouraging independent thinking, intellectual curiosity and analytical reasoning. It aims to develop confident individuals who can apply mathematical knowledge to academic, professional and real-world challenges.

Department Mission

Our Mission

To provide the tools to describe and analyse the physical world, from basic geometry to advanced calculus and statistics.

Our Educational Commitment

  • Develop strong mathematical knowledge and conceptual understanding.
  • Encourage logical reasoning, critical thinking and effective problem-solving abilities.
  • Provide students with mathematical tools to understand and analyse real-world situations.
  • Strengthen analytical and computational skills through mathematical learning.
  • Promote participation in seminars, conferences, workshops and research activities.
  • Encourage lifelong learning and the application of mathematics in science, technology and society.

Programmes Offered

The Department of Mathematics offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes designed to develop mathematical knowledge, analytical ability and problem-solving skills. These programmes support progression towards academic, educational, financial, technological and quantitative careers.

  • B.Sc. Mathematics
  • M.Sc. Mathematics

Academic Pathway

Students begin with foundational mathematical concepts and methods at undergraduate level, then progress to advanced topics and deeper analytical study at postgraduate level, subject to current admission and eligibility requirements.

B.Sc. Mathematics

About the Course

A three-year undergraduate programme developing knowledge of algebra, calculus, differential equations, geometry, statistics and related mathematical methods. It strengthens structured reasoning and the ability to model and solve quantitative problems.

Learning Focus

  • Algebra, calculus and differential equations
  • Geometry, discrete mathematics and numerical methods
  • Probability, statistics and mathematical modelling
  • Computational tools and problem-solving

Career Opportunities

  • Banking, insurance and financial-services roles
  • Data, reporting and operations analysis
  • Teaching after completing required professional qualifications
  • Actuarial and quantitative pathways with further training
  • Competitive examinations and public services
  • Postgraduate study in mathematics, statistics, data science or computing

M.Sc. Mathematics

About the Course

A postgraduate programme offering advanced study of pure and applied mathematics. It develops proof, abstraction, modelling, analysis and research skills for academic, educational and quantitative professions.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced algebra and analysis
  • Differential equations and mathematical modelling
  • Topology, numerical methods and applied mathematics
  • Research methodology, seminars and project work

Career Opportunities

  • College teaching after meeting eligibility requirements
  • Research and project-associate roles
  • Data, analytics and quantitative support
  • Banking, insurance and competitive examinations
  • Scientific computing and modelling with additional technical skills
  • Doctoral study and academic research
