Department of Botany

About the Department

The Department of Botany is dedicated to providing quality education in plant science and developing scientific knowledge, practical skills and environmental awareness among students. The department encourages students to explore plant diversity, understand plant life processes and learn about the importance of plants in our daily lives.

Through classroom teaching, laboratory experiments, field visits, seminars and project work, students gain both theoretical knowledge and practical experience. The department promotes scientific observation, analytical thinking, research aptitude and an appreciation of biodiversity and environmental conservation.

Department Highlights

  • Quality education in plant sciences.
  • Undergraduate and postgraduate Botany programmes.
  • Practical laboratory and field-based learning.
  • Plant identification, taxonomy and physiology.
  • Biodiversity and environmental conservation awareness.
  • Seminars, workshops and academic activities.
  • Research-oriented and application-based learning.
  • Career guidance and higher education opportunities.

Vision & Mission

Department Vision

To achieve excellence in botanical education and research by developing knowledgeable, skilled and environmentally responsible graduates with an understanding of plant diversity, conservation and sustainable development.

Department Mission

To provide quality education in Botany through effective teaching, practical laboratory training, field-based learning and research activities. To encourage scientific thinking, teamwork, innovation and awareness of biodiversity and environmental conservation.

  • Provide strong theoretical and practical knowledge in plant sciences.
  • Develop scientific thinking, observation and analytical skills.
  • Promote biodiversity awareness and environmental conservation.
  • Encourage research, innovation and independent learning.
  • Prepare students for higher education and professional opportunities.

Department Objectives

  • To develop students' skills in plant identification, morphology, anatomy, taxonomy and physiology.
  • To provide practical knowledge of plant science through laboratory experiments and field studies.
  • To create awareness about biodiversity, medicinal plants and environmental conservation.
  • To encourage research activities, critical thinking and scientific exploration.
  • To promote the sustainable use and conservation of plant resources.
  • To prepare students for higher education, research, teaching and career opportunities in life sciences.

Programmes Offered

Undergraduate Programme

  • B.Sc. Botany

    An undergraduate programme focusing on plant diversity, structure, physiology, genetics, ecology, taxonomy and the economic importance of plants. The programme develops scientific knowledge and practical laboratory skills.

Postgraduate Programme

  • M.Sc. Botany

    A postgraduate programme providing advanced knowledge of plant sciences, research methods, ecology, physiology, taxonomy and applied biology. It encourages independent research and scientific investigation.

B.Sc. Botany

About the Course

B.Sc. Botany is an undergraduate programme that provides comprehensive knowledge of plant diversity, structure, physiology, genetics, ecology, taxonomy and the economic uses of plants. Practical learning develops observation, identification, analytical and laboratory skills.

Learning Focus

  • Plant diversity, taxonomy and anatomy.
  • Plant physiology, genetics and biotechnology basics.
  • Ecology, biodiversity and conservation.
  • Plant pathology and economic botany.
  • Medicinal and economically important plants.
  • Practical laboratory and field studies.
  • Plant identification and scientific observation.

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory and research assistant.
  • Herbarium and field assistant.
  • Nursery and horticulture management.
  • Seed production and quality testing.
  • Environmental and biodiversity projects.
  • Agriculture and plantation sectors.
  • Teaching after the required qualifications.
  • Higher studies in Botany and allied life sciences.

M.Sc. Botany

About the Course

M.Sc. Botany is a postgraduate programme offering advanced knowledge of plant science, research methods, ecology, physiology, taxonomy and applied biology. It prepares students to investigate plant systems and contribute to biodiversity conservation, agriculture and biotechnology.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced plant physiology, taxonomy and genetics.
  • Ecology, conservation and environmental biology.
  • Plant biotechnology and applied botany.
  • Research methodology and scientific investigation.
  • Seminars, fieldwork and project studies.
  • Plant systems and biological resource conservation.

Career Opportunities

  • Research assistant and project associate.
  • Botanical gardens and herbaria.
  • Conservation organisations.
  • Agricultural and biotechnology sectors.
  • Environmental consultancy.
  • Biodiversity documentation and research.
  • Teaching after meeting eligibility requirements.
  • Doctoral research in Botany and allied life sciences.
