Department of Botany

About the Department

Established in 2016, the Department of Botany provides teaching and laboratory experience in plant biology, ecology, taxonomy, physiology and applied plant science. It connects plant knowledge with agriculture, health, biodiversity, industry and environmental sustainability.

The department encourages students to understand plant diversity, structure, function and relationships with the environment. The curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with practical observation, plant identification, laboratory exercises and field-based learning.

Students develop scientific observation, identification, analytical and research skills that support the responsible use and conservation of biological resources.

Department Highlights

  • Established in 2016.
  • Offers B.Sc. Botany.
  • Offers M.Sc. Botany.
  • Plant biology, taxonomy and physiology.
  • Ecology, biodiversity and conservation.
  • Laboratory and field-based learning.
  • Applied plant science and research awareness.

Vision & Mission

Department Vision

To advance plant-science education, research and outreach for the responsible use and conservation of biological resources.

Department Mission

  • Provide strong theoretical and practical learning in plant science.
  • Develop graduates and researchers who can address agricultural, health and environmental challenges.
  • Promote biodiversity awareness, conservation and sustainable practice.
  • Encourage innovation, research and community outreach.

Programmes Offered

Undergraduate Programme

  • B.Sc. Botany

    A three-year undergraduate programme covering plant diversity, structure, physiology, genetics, ecology, taxonomy and the economic uses of plants.

Postgraduate Programme

  • M.Sc. Botany

    A postgraduate programme providing advanced study of plant science, research methods, ecology, physiology, taxonomy and applied biology.

B.Sc. Botany

About the Course

B.Sc. Botany is a three-year undergraduate programme studying plant diversity, structure, physiology, genetics, ecology, taxonomy and economic uses. Practical work develops observation, identification and laboratory skills.

Learning Focus

  • Plant diversity, taxonomy and anatomy.
  • Plant physiology, genetics and biotechnology basics.
  • Ecology, biodiversity and conservation.
  • Plant pathology and economic botany.
  • Practical laboratory and field study.
  • Plant identification and scientific observation.

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory, herbarium or field assistant.
  • Nursery, horticulture and seed-sector roles.
  • Environmental and biodiversity projects.
  • Agriculture, plantation and botanical-support services.
  • Teaching after the required professional qualifications.
  • M.Sc. Botany, biotechnology, ecology or environmental science.

M.Sc. Botany

About the Course

M.Sc. Botany is a postgraduate programme for advanced study of plant science, research methods, ecology, physiology, taxonomy and applied biology. It prepares students to investigate plant systems and contribute to conservation, agriculture and biotechnology.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced plant physiology, taxonomy and genetics.
  • Ecology, conservation and environmental biology.
  • Plant biotechnology and applied botany.
  • Research methods and scientific investigation.
  • Seminars, fieldwork and project study.
  • Plant systems and biological resource conservation.

Career Opportunities

  • Research assistant or project associate.
  • Botanical gardens, herbaria and conservation organizations.
  • Agricultural, seed, nursery and biotechnology sectors.
  • Environmental consultancy and biodiversity documentation.
  • Teaching after meeting eligibility requirements.
  • Doctoral research in botany and allied life sciences.
