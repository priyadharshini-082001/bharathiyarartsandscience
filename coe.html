Controller of Examinations

The Controller of Examinations office coordinates the examination system, assessment procedures, publication of results and academic records. It strives to maintain transparency, accuracy, confidentiality and academic integrity throughout the examination process.

Introduction

The Controller of Examinations (COE) is responsible for planning, coordinating and administering the institution's examination activities in accordance with applicable academic regulations. The office facilitates examination notifications, timetable preparation, assessment coordination, result processing and the maintenance of student examination records.

The office works with departments, faculty members and academic administrators to support a fair, systematic and student-friendly examination process. Students are encouraged to follow official notifications, observe examination rules and submit applications within the prescribed deadlines.
