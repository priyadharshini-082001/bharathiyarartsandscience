About the Department

Discover Life. Explore Nature. Create the Future.

The Department of Zoology is a dynamic centre for learning, discovery, and scientific exploration. Our department introduces students to the fascinating world of animals, microorganisms, ecosystems, and the processes that make life possible. Zoology is not simply the study of animals. It is the study of life, diversity, behaviour, adaptation, evolution, and the relationship between living organisms and their environment. Our department encourages students to look beyond the classroom and understand the living world through observation, experimentation, field studies, and research. The Department of Zoology provides an engaging academic environment where students can develop knowledge, practical skills, creativity, and scientific curiosity. We combine fundamental concepts of biology with modern approaches in life sciences to make learning meaningful and relevant.

Fascinating Areas

Students are introduced to a wide range of fascinating areas of Zoology, including:

  • Animal Diversity and Taxonomy
  • Cell Biology
  • Genetics and Evolution
  • Animal Physiology
  • Developmental Biology
  • Ecology and Environmental Science
  • Biodiversity and Conservation
  • Wildlife Biology
  • Microbiology
  • Molecular Biology
  • Biotechnology
  • Animal Behaviour
  • Immunology
  • Entomology
  • Fisheries and Aquatic Biology

Learning Beyond the Classroom

We believe that the natural world itself is a laboratory. Field visits and educational tours allow students to observe animals, plants, habitats, ecosystems, and biodiversity in their natural surroundings.

Through field-based learning, students develop observation skills and gain a deeper understanding of ecosystems, wildlife, environmental challenges, and conservation practices.

Practical Learning

Our laboratories provide students with opportunities to transform theoretical knowledge into practical experience. Students learn scientific techniques through specimen observation, microscopy, experiments, identification, analysis, and project work.

Practical learning helps students develop:

  • Scientific observation
  • Laboratory skills
  • Problem-solving ability
  • Data collection and analysis
  • Research interest
  • Scientific communication

Research and Innovation

The department encourages students to develop a research mindset from an early stage. Students are motivated to ask questions, investigate problems, collect information, and develop scientific solutions. Research-oriented activities may focus on areas such as biodiversity, ecology, animal behaviour, environmental protection, microbiology, biotechnology, and conservation.

We believe that every question can become the beginning of a discovery.

Nature and Conservation

The Department of Zoology promotes awareness about biodiversity and environmental sustainability. Students are encouraged to understand the importance of protecting wildlife, habitats, natural resources, and ecosystems.

Our activities aim to create environmentally responsible citizens who understand that protecting nature means protecting our own future.

Student Development

Education is not limited to academic knowledge. We encourage students to develop communication, leadership, teamwork, creativity, and presentation skills.

Students can participate in:

  • Seminars and workshops
  • Quiz and science competitions
  • Field visits and study tours
  • Project work
  • Awareness programmes
  • Science exhibitions
  • Conferences and academic events
  • Environmental and extension activities

These experiences help students become confident and responsible professionals.

Our Faculty

Our faculty members are committed to providing effective teaching, academic guidance, mentoring, and individual support. They encourage students to explore new ideas, participate actively in academic activities, and develop an interest in research and higher education.

The department strives to create a friendly learning environment where every student is encouraged to learn, question, explore, and grow.

Career Opportunities

A background in Zoology can open doors to a variety of higher education and career pathways. Students can pursue further studies and careers in areas such as:

  • Higher education and teaching
  • Research and scientific studies
  • Wildlife and biodiversity conservation
  • Environmental science
  • Biotechnology
  • Microbiology
  • Fisheries and aquaculture
  • Laboratory and diagnostic fields
  • Public health and allied sciences
  • Government and environmental organisations
  • Animal and wildlife-related sectors

Higher education can also lead students towards specialised fields in biological and life sciences.

The Department of Zoology is more than a place to study animals. It is a place to observe, question, discover, innovate, and make a difference.
