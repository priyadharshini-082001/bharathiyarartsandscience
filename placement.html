WELCOME TO OUR CELL

About Us

The Placement & Career Guidance Cell is dedicated to helping students build successful careers. We strive to bridge the gap between academic training and industry expectations, empowering both UG and PG final-year students to face the professional world with confidence and competence.

Building Careers. Creating Opportunities.
OUR COMMITMENT

What We Do

01

Campus Placement Drives

We organize annual campus recruitment drives for final-year undergraduate and postgraduate students. From coordination with companies to logistical support, we ensure every stage of the placement process runs smoothly.

02

Industry Engagement

We invite corporate speakers to interact with students, explaining current market trends, industry expectations, and emerging career possibilities.

03

Skill Enhancement Training

Intensive training sessions are provided in areas such as aptitude tests, resume writing, interview techniques, and group discussions to prepare students for selection committees.

04

Career & Soft-Skill Development

We regularly arrange workshops on soft skills, career planning, personality development and related topics. Industry experts are brought in to enrich student learning with real-world insights.

05

Counselling & Guidance

We support students individually and in groups to clarify career goals, overcome challenges, and develop strategies for success.

Learn Today. Lead Tomorrow.
OUR PURPOSE

Objectives of the Placement Cell

Career Counseling & Development

Provide one-on-one career counseling and development sessions for students and graduates. Help students explore career options and plan their professional paths effectively. Identify and address individual student needs related to career growth and decision-making.

Career Services & Training Programs

Develop career services programs that support students in career exploration and preparation. Organize free pre-placement training focused on aptitude tests, resume writing, interview techniques and group discussions. Conduct workshops, expert lectures, and interactive presentations on career planning and employability skills.

Recruitment & Placement Support

Organize campus placement drives and on-campus Mega job fairs for final-year UG, PG and also for outgoing students. Find, inform, and manage pool placement opportunities with partnering institutions. Maintain regular communication with potential employers, manage recruiter correspondence and feedback, and ensure recruitment opportunities are accessible to all students.

Industry Collaboration

Build and strengthen ties between industry and academia through regular engagement. Invite industry experts for guest lectures, seminars, and panel discussions. Foster collaboration that aligns academic skills with real-world industry demands.
