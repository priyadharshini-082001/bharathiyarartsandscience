DISCOVER OUR DEPARTMENT

Nourishing Lives, Building Healthier Communities

Established in 2017, the Department of Nutrition & Dietetics studies the relationship among food, nutrients, health, disease prevention and human development. It combines nutrition science, food preparation, community awareness, counselling and entrepreneurship.

The department encourages students to understand healthy food choices, balanced diets, food safety and nutrition across the life cycle through academic learning and practical activities.

OUR VISION

To contribute to healthy people and food-secure communities through responsible nutrition education and practice.

OUR MISSION
  • Build scientific understanding of nutrition across the life cycle.
  • Develop skills in diet planning, food service and community education.
  • Promote research-based interventions and healthy food choices.
  • Encourage leadership, social engagement and nutrition entrepreneurship.

ACADEMIC PATHWAY

Programmes Offered

The Department of Nutrition & Dietetics offers undergraduate and postgraduate learning in human nutrition, food science, dietetics, food safety, community nutrition and research. Students develop knowledge and practical skills for professional, community and research-oriented opportunities.

01 UNDERGRADUATE
B.Sc.

B.Sc. Nutrition & Dietetics

A three-year undergraduate programme covering human nutrition, food science, physiology, therapeutic diets, community nutrition and food-service management. Students learn to plan balanced diets and communicate practical health guidance.

Learning Focus

  • Human nutrition and physiology
  • Food science, preparation and safety
  • Dietetics and therapeutic nutrition
  • Community nutrition and counselling
  • Food-service management

Career Opportunities

  • Nutrition or wellness assistant
  • Dietary and food-service support in hospitals or institutions
  • Community nutrition and public-health projects
  • Food-product, quality and nutrition-labelling roles
  • Fitness, wellness and health-education services within professional scope
  • Postgraduate study for advanced dietetics and specialist roles
View Syllabus ↗
02 POSTGRADUATE
M.Sc.

M.Sc. Food & Nutrition

A postgraduate programme for advanced study of nutrition science, food systems, research, clinical applications and community nutrition. It develops assessment, intervention, communication and research skills for responsible professional practice.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced human and clinical nutrition
  • Food science, safety and product development
  • Community and public-health nutrition
  • Research methods and nutritional assessment
  • Counselling and project work

Career Opportunities

  • Nutritionist or dietetics professional, subject to applicable qualifications
  • Clinical, community and public-health nutrition
  • Food-industry research, quality and product development
  • Wellness, sports-nutrition and health-education services
  • Teaching and research after meeting eligibility norms
  • Doctoral study and advanced nutrition research
View Syllabus ↗
03 APPLIED LEARNING
Health

Nutrition & Community Wellness

Practical learning and community activities help students understand healthy eating, food safety, balanced meal planning and nutrition awareness across different stages of life.

Learning Opportunities

  • Balanced meal and diet planning
  • Food preparation and hygiene practices
  • Nutrition education and awareness programmes
  • Community projects and food-service activities

Career Opportunities

  • Nutrition education and wellness support
  • Community nutrition initiatives
  • Food-service and dietary support
  • Food quality and nutrition communication
  • Health-promotion projects
  • Further study in nutrition and allied health sciences
