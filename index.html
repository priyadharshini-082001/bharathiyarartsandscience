Welcome to BASCW

Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women was established in 1997 by Sri Sakthi Educational Trust with a commitment to expanding higher-education opportunities for women, particularly learners from rural communities. Affiliated to Periyar University, the college offers a supportive academic environment where knowledge, values, skills and social responsibility develop together.
