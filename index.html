ABOUT OUR COLLEGE

Welcome to

Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women (Autonomous)

Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women is committed to providing quality higher education with knowledge, values, skills and social responsibility. Established in 1997 by Sri Sakthi Educational Trust, the college focuses on empowering women and supporting the educational aspirations of rural communities. Affiliated to Periyar University, the institution offers a supportive academic environment with opportunities for learning, research, innovation and overall development.
