Welcome to Bharathiyar Arts and Science College for Women

Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women was established in 1997 by Sri Sakthi Educational Trust with a commitment to expanding higher-education opportunities for women, particularly learners from rural communities. Affiliated to Periyar University, the college offers a supportive academic environment where knowledge, values, skills and social responsibility develop together.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Vision, Mission & Motto

Vision

To be a centre of excellence in higher education that empowers young women with knowledge, skills, values and confidence to become responsible and successful citizens of society.

Mission

To provide quality and inclusive education through innovative teaching, practical learning and value-based education while nurturing creativity, leadership, employability and lifelong learning.

Motto

Inspiring every student to learn, lead and contribute meaningfully to society with confidence, compassion and integrity.

Why Choose Bharathiyar Arts and Science College for Women?

Empowering Education with Discipline and Progress.

BASCW provides a vibrant and supportive learning environment where students can build strong academic foundations, develop professional skills and grow into confident, responsible leaders. With experienced faculty, modern infrastructure, diverse programmes, career support, research opportunities and extensive co-curricular activities, the institution focuses on academic excellence and holistic development.
