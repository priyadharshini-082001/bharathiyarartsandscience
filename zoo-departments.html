DISCOVER OUR DEPARTMENT

Exploring Animal Life, Protecting Our Natural World

Established in 2015 and expanded with postgraduate study in 2018, the Department of Zoology explores animal diversity, physiology, ecology, genetics and applied life science. The department connects zoology with biotechnology, biochemistry, microbiology, human genetics and environmental studies.

Through classroom learning, laboratory investigations and field-based experiences, students develop scientific observation, analytical thinking and an understanding of animal life, biodiversity and environmental conservation.

OUR VISION

To achieve excellence in animal-science learning, teaching and research while developing respect for nature, ethics and scientific practice.

OUR MISSION
  • Provide strong conceptual and practical education in zoology.
  • Develop scientific observation, laboratory and field-study skills.
  • Promote biodiversity, ethics and environmental responsibility.
  • Empower rural women through advanced life-science education and research.

ACADEMIC PATHWAY

Programmes Offered

The Department of Zoology offers undergraduate and postgraduate education in animal science. Students explore animal diversity, anatomy, physiology, genetics, ecology, evolution and applied biological sciences through theoretical learning, practical work and research-oriented activities.

01 UNDERGRADUATE
B.Sc.

B.Sc. Zoology

A three-year undergraduate programme covering animal diversity, anatomy, physiology, genetics, ecology, evolution and applied zoology. Laboratory and field experiences help students develop observation, classification and scientific-analysis skills.

Learning Focus

  • Invertebrate and vertebrate diversity
  • Animal physiology, genetics and evolution
  • Ecology, biodiversity and conservation
  • Applied zoology, biotechnology basics and practical work

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory or field assistant
  • Wildlife, biodiversity and conservation projects
  • Aquaculture, sericulture and related applied sectors
  • Environmental education and outreach
  • Teaching after the required professional qualification
  • M.Sc. Zoology, wildlife biology, biotechnology or allied sciences
View Syllabus ↗
02 POSTGRADUATE
M.Sc.

M.Sc. Zoology

A postgraduate programme providing advanced learning in animal science, ecology, physiology, genetics and research. Students develop the ability to design investigations, interpret biological evidence and contribute to conservation, health and applied life sciences.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced physiology, genetics and developmental biology
  • Ecology, conservation and environmental zoology
  • Biotechnology, microbiology and interdisciplinary applications
  • Research methodology, seminars, fieldwork and project study

Career Opportunities

  • Research assistant or project associate
  • Wildlife, environmental and biodiversity organizations
  • Life-science laboratories and applied biological sectors
  • Teaching after meeting applicable eligibility requirements
  • Scientific documentation and conservation education
  • Doctoral research in zoology and allied disciplines
Explore Research Resources ↗
03 APPLIED LEARNING
ECO

Applied Zoology & Conservation

Applied zoology connects animal-science knowledge with biodiversity protection, environmental management, wildlife studies and sustainable biological practices. Students explore the relevance of zoology to ecosystems and society.

Learning Opportunities

  • Animal diversity and ecological relationships
  • Biodiversity assessment and conservation concepts
  • Wildlife, environmental and field-study methods
  • Applied life science and project-based learning

Career Opportunities

  • Biodiversity and conservation project support
  • Wildlife and environmental field assistance
  • Aquaculture and sericulture-related opportunities
  • Environmental awareness and education
  • Research internships and project assistance
  • Further study in wildlife biology and allied sciences
