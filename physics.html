About the Department

Established in 2003, the Department of Physics offers undergraduate and postgraduate learning supported by practical work and faculty guidance. It develops understanding of matter, energy, measurement, instrumentation and the physical principles behind modern technology. The department encourages scientific curiosity, analytical thinking and experimental learning through theoretical instruction and laboratory practice.

Academic Excellence

The department promotes a strong foundation in classical and modern physics through conceptual learning, demonstrations, experiments and problem-solving activities. Students explore mechanics, electricity, magnetism, optics, electronics, thermal physics and quantum concepts. Practical sessions strengthen measurement accuracy, laboratory skills and the ability to interpret experimental observations.

Programmes and Opportunities

The department offers B.Sc. Physics and M.Sc. Physics programmes to support progressive learning in physical sciences. Students develop theoretical knowledge, experimental competence and research awareness while preparing for opportunities in scientific laboratories, education, instrumentation, electronics, technical services and advanced research.

Department Vision

Our Vision

To motivate women learners to become competent physics professionals and lifelong scientific thinkers.

Our Commitment

The department aspires to develop scientifically informed, confident and capable graduates through quality education, practical experimentation and research-oriented learning. Students are encouraged to question, investigate and understand the physical world while applying scientific principles to technological developments and real-world challenges.

Department Mission

Our Mission

  • Teach physics through clear concepts, experiments and problem-solving.
  • Use innovative methods to connect theory with application.
  • Build laboratory, analytical and research capability.
  • Prepare students for advanced study, education and technology-oriented work.

Learning Through Scientific Exploration

The department integrates theoretical instruction with laboratory experiments, demonstrations and analytical activities. Students are encouraged to develop logical reasoning, quantitative skills, experimental accuracy and independent thinking. The learning environment supports scientific investigation and prepares students to apply physics knowledge in academic, research and technology-oriented settings.

Programmes Offered

The Department of Physics provides undergraduate and postgraduate education in physical sciences. The programmes combine conceptual understanding, mathematical analysis and experimental practice to develop a strong foundation for scientific study and professional growth.

  • B.Sc. Physics
  • M.Sc. Physics

Academic Pathway

Students can begin with undergraduate study in physics and progress to postgraduate specialisation. The programmes support further learning in physics, electronics, materials science and applied sciences, along with opportunities in teaching, laboratory work, instrumentation and research, subject to applicable eligibility requirements.

B.Sc. Physics

About the Course

A three-year undergraduate programme covering mechanics, electricity, magnetism, optics, electronics, thermal physics, quantum concepts and practical measurement. The programme builds scientific reasoning and experimental accuracy through theoretical study, laboratory exercises and problem-solving activities.

Learning Focus

  • Mechanics, waves, optics and thermal physics
  • Electricity, magnetism and electronics
  • Modern, atomic and quantum physics
  • Laboratory measurement, instrumentation and computation

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory or instrumentation assistant
  • Electronics, energy, optics and technical-support roles
  • Quality testing and measurement services
  • Teaching after the required professional qualification
  • Scientific and technical competitive examinations
  • M.Sc. Physics, electronics, materials or applied science

M.Sc. Physics

About the Course

A postgraduate programme that advances theoretical, experimental and applied physics. Students strengthen mathematical analysis, instrumentation, research practice and the ability to investigate complex physical systems. The programme develops advanced subject knowledge and supports further academic, scientific and technology-oriented pursuits.

Learning Focus

  • Classical, quantum and statistical physics
  • Electromagnetism, electronics and solid-state physics
  • Instrumentation, computational methods and advanced experiments
  • Research methodology, seminars and project work

Career Opportunities

  • Research assistant or project associate
  • Instrumentation, electronics and testing roles
  • Scientific laboratories and technical organizations
  • Teaching after meeting applicable eligibility norms
  • Data and computational roles with additional skills
  • Ph.D. and advanced research in physics or materials science
