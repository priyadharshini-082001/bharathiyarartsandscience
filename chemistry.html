Department of Chemistry

About the Department

Chemistry is the Most Excellent Child of Intellect and Art

– Sir Cyril Norman Hinshelwood

The Department of Chemistry is an integral part of Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women, dedicated to providing quality education in chemical sciences and developing scientific knowledge among students. Chemistry is a fundamental science that plays a significant role in medicine, agriculture, industry, environmental protection, food science and technological advancement. Established in 2003 and developed as a research department from 2014, the Department of Chemistry combines conceptual learning with laboratory practice. It supports rural women in building scientific curiosity, accuracy, research orientation and practical competence for higher education and industry. The department aims to provide a strong foundation in chemical principles through a balanced combination of theoretical instruction and practical laboratory training. It encourages students to develop scientific curiosity, analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities and research aptitude. The Department of Chemistry focuses on the systematic understanding of the major branches of chemistry, including Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, Physical Chemistry, Analytical Chemistry and Environmental Chemistry. The curriculum provides students with knowledge of chemical reactions, molecular structures, chemical bonding, thermodynamics, reaction mechanisms, instrumental methods of analysis and the applications of chemistry in everyday life. Classroom teaching is supported by assignments, seminars, presentations, group discussions, demonstrations and practical sessions. The department encourages student-centred learning and continuous academic improvement. Students are motivated to participate actively in academic activities, develop independent learning habits and apply scientific concepts to real-life situations. Practical education is an essential component of chemistry. Laboratory sessions provide opportunities for students to develop experimental skills, accuracy, observation, interpretation of results and scientific documentation. Students gain experience in qualitative and quantitative analysis, volumetric analysis, preparation of chemical compounds, chemical estimations and other experiments prescribed in the curriculum. Appropriate emphasis is placed on laboratory safety, careful handling of chemicals, waste disposal and ethical scientific practices. The department encourages the use of modern and effective teaching methodologies to improve student engagement and learning outcomes. These include ICT-enabled teaching, PowerPoint presentations, laboratory demonstrations, student seminars, assignments, quizzes, project work and collaborative learning. Students are encouraged to participate in academic competitions, conferences, workshops, guest lectures and other educational programmes to broaden their knowledge and develop communication, teamwork, leadership and presentation skills. The department promotes scientific curiosity and research aptitude among students. Project work, experimental investigations, seminars and scientific discussions help students understand research methods and develop independent thinking. Students are encouraged to explore emerging areas such as Green Chemistry, Nanotechnology, Polymer Chemistry, Medicinal Chemistry, Materials Science and Environmental Chemistry. These areas highlight the importance of chemistry in addressing contemporary scientific, industrial and environmental challenges. Skill development programmes form an important part of the department's academic activities. Workshops, certificate courses, hands-on training, industrial visits and guest lectures help students gain practical knowledge and improve their professional competencies. Environmental awareness programmes and activities related to water conservation, waste management, pollution prevention and sustainable resource utilisation encourage students to become environmentally responsible citizens. The department also supports career guidance and motivates students to acquire the technical and soft skills necessary for employment and higher education.

Career Opportunities

Chemistry graduates have opportunities in various fields, depending on their qualifications, experience and specialised training. Career pathways include:

  • Chemical Industries: Production, processing and quality control.
  • Pharmaceutical Industries: Quality assurance, quality control and analytical support.
  • Research Laboratories: Laboratory analysis and research assistance.
  • Environmental Laboratories: Water testing, pollution analysis and environmental monitoring.
  • Food and Cosmetic Industries: Product testing and quality assessment.
  • Higher Education: Postgraduate studies, research and academic careers with the required qualifications.
  • Government and Competitive Examinations: Eligible scientific, technical and administrative positions.
  • Entrepreneurship: Small-scale enterprises and chemistry-related businesses, subject to applicable regulations.

The department encourages students to identify suitable career paths and pursue opportunities aligned with their interests and academic strengths.

The department strives to produce competent graduates who contribute to scientific advancement, industrial development, environmental sustainability and the progress of society.

Department Vision

Our Vision

To emerge as a centre of excellence in chemical education by imparting quality education, promoting scientific research, fostering innovation and developing competent, ethical and socially responsible graduates who contribute to scientific advancement and sustainable development.

The Department of Chemistry envisions creating a dynamic academic environment that encourages curiosity, creativity, critical thinking and lifelong learning. It aims to provide students with a strong foundation in chemical sciences and the practical skills required to meet the challenges of higher education, research, industry and society.

The department aspires to nurture scientifically knowledgeable individuals who can apply chemical principles to real-world problems and contribute to the advancement of science and technology.

Vision Objectives

  • To provide comprehensive knowledge in the fundamental and advanced concepts of chemistry through effective teaching and learning practices.
  • To cultivate scientific curiosity, logical reasoning, analytical abilities and problem-solving skills.
  • To encourage research-oriented learning, experimentation, creativity and innovation.
  • To develop laboratory skills, experimental accuracy, observation, data analysis and interpretation.
  • To promote environmental awareness and sustainable chemical practices, including green chemistry, pollution prevention and waste minimisation.
  • To prepare students for higher education, research, industry, entrepreneurship and other professional opportunities.
  • To instil integrity, discipline, teamwork, scientific ethics and social responsibility.
  • To empower students with knowledge, confidence and independent thinking so that they become responsible citizens and active contributors to national development.

Department Mission

Our Mission

The mission of the Department of Chemistry is to provide quality chemical education through innovative teaching methodologies, effective laboratory training, research-oriented activities and skill development programmes. The department is committed to nurturing knowledgeable, competent, ethical and environmentally conscious graduates who can contribute effectively to academia, industry, research and society.

Mission Objectives

  • To deliver a comprehensive and systematic understanding of chemistry through student-centred teaching, updated learning resources, interactive classroom practices and continuous academic assessment.
  • To integrate classroom instruction with laboratory experiments, demonstrations, project work and practical applications.
  • To encourage students to participate in seminars, workshops, scientific discussions, projects and research-oriented activities.
  • To provide opportunities for students to acquire laboratory techniques, analytical methods, data interpretation skills, scientific documentation and problem-solving abilities.
  • To sensitise students to environmental challenges and promote eco-friendly chemical practices, safe laboratory procedures, efficient resource utilisation and sustainable waste management.
  • To organise skill development programmes, industrial exposure, guest lectures, internships and career guidance activities, wherever feasible.
  • To develop honesty, responsibility, teamwork, leadership, discipline and adherence to scientific ethics and laboratory safety standards.
  • To motivate students to pursue advanced studies, professional training, continuous learning and engagement with emerging developments in chemistry.
  • To encourage students to apply their scientific knowledge to community welfare through environmental awareness, science outreach and activities addressing societal needs.
  • To strengthen the quality of teaching, learning, practical training and departmental activities through feedback, academic review and quality-enhancement initiatives.

Programmes Offered

Academic Programmes

The Department of Chemistry offers academic programmes that combine theoretical knowledge, laboratory practice and scientific applications. The programmes aim to develop conceptual understanding, practical competence, analytical skills and research awareness.

  • B.Sc. Chemistry

    A three-year undergraduate programme introducing students to the major branches of chemistry, practical experimentation, analytical methods and scientific applications.

  • M.Sc. Chemistry

    A postgraduate programme providing advanced theoretical knowledge, laboratory experience, research methodology and preparation for professional opportunities.

B.Sc. Chemistry

About the Course

B.Sc. Chemistry is an undergraduate science programme covering the principles and practical applications of Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, Physical Chemistry and Analytical Chemistry. Students gain laboratory discipline, observation, measurement, problem-solving and scientific-reporting skills.

Learning Focus

  • Organic, inorganic and physical chemistry.
  • Analytical methods and quantitative techniques.
  • Laboratory safety, instrumentation and practical work.
  • Environmental, industrial and applied chemistry.
  • Scientific observation, measurement and reporting.
  • Chemical reactions, bonding and molecular structures.
  • Qualitative and quantitative analysis.

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory technician or quality-control assistant.
  • Opportunities in chemical, pharmaceutical, food, textile and materials industries.
  • Water, environmental and testing laboratories.
  • Technical sales and scientific-support roles.
  • Teaching after obtaining the required professional qualifications.
  • M.Sc. Chemistry and specialised postgraduate study.

M.Sc. Chemistry

About the Course

M.Sc. Chemistry is a postgraduate programme offering advanced theoretical and laboratory study in the major branches of chemistry. It develops deeper analytical ability, research readiness, instrumentation awareness and the capacity to solve chemical problems relevant to industry and society.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced organic, inorganic and physical chemistry.
  • Spectroscopy, instrumentation and analytical techniques.
  • Research methodology, seminars and project work.
  • Industrial, environmental and materials applications.
  • Advanced experimentation, interpretation of results and scientific communication.
  • Scientific documentation and research presentation.

Career Opportunities

  • Chemist, analyst or quality-control professional.
  • Pharmaceutical, chemical, polymer, food and environmental industries.
  • Research assistant or project associate.
  • Teaching after meeting the applicable eligibility requirements.
  • Regulatory, documentation and laboratory-management roles.
  • Ph.D. and advanced research programmes.

Laboratory Infrastructure

Chemistry Laboratories

The Department of Chemistry has three well-equipped laboratories that support practical learning, experimentation and analytical training. These facilities help students connect theoretical concepts with practical applications.

The laboratories have the necessary equipment for the preparation of samples and standards used in the analysis of liquid and solid materials. Students gain practical exposure to laboratory procedures, experimental techniques and scientific observations.

  • Three equipped laboratories for practical training.
  • Facilities for sample and standard preparation.
  • Analysis of liquid and solid samples.
  • Qualitative and quantitative experimental procedures.
  • Practical training in chemical analysis and estimations.
  • Emphasis on laboratory safety and responsible chemical handling.
  • Development of observation, accuracy and analytical skills.
