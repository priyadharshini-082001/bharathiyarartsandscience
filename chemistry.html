Department of Chemistry

About the Department

Established in 2003 and developed as a research department from 2014, the Department of Chemistry combines conceptual learning with laboratory practice. It supports rural women in building scientific curiosity, accuracy, research orientation and practical competence for higher education and industry.

The department encourages students to understand chemical principles through classroom instruction, practical experiments, observation, analysis and scientific enquiry. Its learning environment helps students develop laboratory discipline, problem-solving abilities and confidence in applying chemistry to real-world situations.

Three equipped laboratories support practical learning, including sample preparation and the analysis of liquid and solid materials. Students are encouraged to develop environmental awareness, research interest and the skills required for scientific and industrial careers.

Department Vision

Our Vision

To develop scientifically informed women graduates with strong laboratory, analytical and research capabilities. The department aims to nurture scientific thinking, practical competence, ethical responsibility and a commitment to lifelong learning.

Department Mission

Our Mission

  • Build a sound understanding of chemical principles and their practical applications.
  • Provide safe, systematic and hands-on laboratory training.
  • Promote research, innovation and environmental responsibility.
  • Prepare students for careers in industry, education and advanced scientific study.
  • Encourage analytical thinking, accurate observation and scientific problem-solving.
  • Develop professional ethics, teamwork and responsible laboratory practices.

Programmes Offered

Academic Programmes

The Department of Chemistry offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes that combine theoretical knowledge, laboratory practice and scientific applications.

  • B.Sc. Chemistry

    A three-year undergraduate programme introducing students to the major branches of chemistry, practical experimentation and analytical methods.

  • M.Sc. Chemistry

    A postgraduate programme providing advanced theoretical knowledge, laboratory experience and preparation for research and professional opportunities.

B.Sc. Chemistry

About the Course

B.Sc. Chemistry is a three-year undergraduate science programme covering the principles and practical applications of organic, inorganic, physical and analytical chemistry. Students gain laboratory discipline, observation, measurement, problem-solving and scientific-reporting skills.

Learning Focus

  • Organic, inorganic and physical chemistry.
  • Analytical methods and quantitative techniques.
  • Laboratory safety, instrumentation and practical work.
  • Environmental, industrial and applied chemistry.
  • Scientific observation, measurement and reporting.

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory technician or quality-control assistant.
  • Opportunities in chemical, pharmaceutical, food, textile and materials industries.
  • Water, environmental and testing laboratories.
  • Technical sales and scientific-support roles.
  • Teaching after obtaining the required professional qualifications.
  • M.Sc. Chemistry and specialised postgraduate study.

M.Sc. Chemistry

About the Course

M.Sc. Chemistry is a postgraduate programme offering advanced theoretical and laboratory study in the major branches of chemistry. It develops deeper analytical ability, research readiness, instrumentation awareness and the capacity to solve chemical problems relevant to industry and society.

Learning Focus

  • Advanced organic, inorganic and physical chemistry.
  • Spectroscopy, instrumentation and analytical techniques.
  • Research methodology, seminars and project work.
  • Industrial, environmental and materials applications.
  • Advanced experimentation, interpretation of results and scientific communication.

Career Opportunities

  • Chemist, analyst or quality-control professional.
  • Pharmaceutical, chemical, polymer, food and environmental industries.
  • Research assistant or project associate.
  • Teaching after meeting the applicable eligibility requirements.
  • Regulatory, documentation and laboratory-management roles.
  • Ph.D. and advanced research programmes.

Lavoisier Club Activities

About the Club

The Lavoisier Club provides opportunities for students to explore chemistry beyond the classroom. Through academic activities and scientific discussions, students can strengthen their curiosity, teamwork, communication and understanding of chemical applications.

Activities

  • Student presentations on chemical concepts.
  • Science awareness and chemistry-based demonstrations.
  • Quiz programmes and academic competitions.
  • Seminars and workshops related to chemistry.
  • Environmental awareness and responsible chemical use.
  • Celebration of important science-related occasions.
