Department of Nutrition & Dietetics

About the Department

Nutrition is more than a science—it is a transformative force for building healthier generations and a better world. The Department of Nutrition and Dietetics, established in 2017, is dedicated to developing skilled professionals and advancing health through the principles of food science and dietetics.

Our department promotes the importance of balanced nutrition and encourages evidence-based food choices. It provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the relationship between food, nutrients, health, disease prevention and human development.

Through academic learning, laboratory experiences, practical training, research activities and community engagement, students develop the knowledge and professional competencies required to promote healthy dietary practices and improve quality of life.

The department offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes designed to prepare students for diverse career opportunities in healthcare, clinical dietetics, nutrition, the food industry, research, education and allied health sectors.

Programmes Offered

  • Undergraduate Programme: B.Sc. Nutrition and Dietetics.
  • Postgraduate Programme: M.Sc. Food and Nutrition.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

The department believes that effective nutrition education extends beyond textbooks and lectures. Students gain opportunities to develop practical competencies through laboratory work, clinical and community-based training, field visits, internships, case studies, dietary assessments, seminars, workshops, student projects and research activities.

These experiences help students assess nutritional needs, interpret scientific information, communicate nutrition concepts effectively and develop appropriate dietary and health interventions.

Vision & Mission

Department Vision

To emerge as a centre of excellence in nutrition and dietetics education, research, and community service, contributing to healthier individuals, sustainable food practices, and improved quality of life.

Department Mission

The Department of Nutrition and Dietetics aims to:

  • Provide strong theoretical and practical foundations in food, nutrition, dietetics, and health sciences.
  • Develop competent professionals who can apply scientific principles of nutrition in clinical, community, industrial, and research settings.
  • Encourage students to make and promote balanced, safe, sustainable, and evidence-based food choices.
  • Foster critical thinking, scientific curiosity, communication skills, and professional ethics.
  • Provide opportunities for practical exposure through laboratory work, clinical training, field visits, internships, projects, and community-based activities.
  • Create awareness about the role of nutrition in the prevention and management of lifestyle-related and nutrition-related disorders.
  • Encourage sustainable practices, including paperless academic and administrative initiatives, responsible food practices, and environmental consciousness.

Academic Programmes

Programmes Offered

The Department of Nutrition and Dietetics offers academic programmes designed to provide students with a strong foundation in theoretical knowledge and practical application.

Promoting Evidence-Based Nutrition

In an era of rapidly changing dietary trends and widespread nutrition information, the department emphasizes the importance of evidence-based nutrition.

Students are encouraged to evaluate nutrition information critically and understand the scientific basis behind dietary recommendations. The department promotes responsible communication of nutrition information and helps students distinguish scientifically supported practices from unverified claims.

This approach develops the ability to make informed recommendations based on scientific evidence, individual requirements, cultural considerations and available resources.

Nutrition for a Healthier Society

Good nutrition is essential at every stage of life. Nutritional requirements vary from infancy and childhood through adolescence, adulthood, pregnancy and older age.

Students gain an understanding of nutrition across the life cycle and learn how nutrition influences growth, development, health, productivity and quality of life.

The department also promotes awareness of major public health concerns, including nutritional deficiencies, undernutrition, overweight and obesity, and diet-related lifestyle conditions.

Sustainability and Responsible Practices

The Department of Nutrition and Dietetics recognizes the connection between food, health and environmental sustainability.

Students are encouraged to consider responsible food choices, food waste reduction, efficient use of resources and sustainable approaches to food and nutrition.

As part of its commitment to a greener academic environment, the department supports paperless practices wherever possible through digital learning resources, electronic communication, digital documentation and technology-enabled academic activities.

Academic Learning Resources

  • Nutrition and Dietetics Textbooks.

  • Diet-Planning and Therapeutic Nutrition Exercises.
  • Food Preparation and Safety Activities.
  • Community Nutrition and Awareness Resources.
  • Research, Project Work and Presentations.
  • Digital Learning Materials and Academic Resources.

Building Professional Competence

The department places equal importance on knowledge, skills, values and professional responsibility. Students are encouraged to develop effective communication, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving, counselling, research and decision-making abilities.

The ultimate goal is to prepare graduates who can confidently apply their knowledge in professional settings while maintaining ethical standards and demonstrating sensitivity to the nutritional, cultural, economic and social needs of the people they serve.
