01 / COMMUNITY SERVICE

National Service Scheme (NSS)

"Not Me, But You"

01
GET TO KNOW US

About NSS

The National Service Scheme provides students with opportunities to participate in community service and social development activities. It encourages young people to understand community needs, develop social awareness and contribute to the welfare of society.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

To develop responsible and socially conscious students through voluntary community service. The programme promotes social harmony, civic responsibility, environmental awareness and active participation in nation-building.

WHAT WE AIM FOR

Objectives

  • Understand the community and its needs.
  • Develop social and civic responsibility.
  • Promote leadership and teamwork.
  • Encourage voluntary community service.
  • Create awareness of social issues.
  • Develop personality through service.
OUR INITIATIVES

Major Activities

  • Tree plantation programmes.
  • Health and hygiene awareness.
  • Blood donation programmes.
  • Road safety campaigns.
  • Literacy awareness.
  • Women empowerment initiatives.

National Cadet Corps (NCC)

"Unity and Discipline"

02
GET TO KNOW US

About NCC

The National Cadet Corps provides students with opportunities to develop discipline, leadership, confidence and a spirit of service. Through training and organised activities, cadets learn teamwork, responsibility and commitment towards the nation.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

To develop disciplined, confident and responsible young citizens with leadership qualities, national integration and a commitment to serve the community and nation.

WHAT WE AIM FOR

Objectives

  • Develop leadership and discipline.
  • Build confidence and self-reliance.
  • Promote national integration.
  • Encourage teamwork and cooperation.
  • Develop physical fitness and endurance.
  • Inspire responsible citizenship.
OUR INITIATIVES

Major Activities

  • Parade and drill training.
  • Physical fitness activities.
  • Leadership and personality development.
  • Adventure and outdoor activities.
  • National celebration participation.
  • Community service activities.

About YRC

The Youth Red Cross encourages students to develop humanitarian values, compassion and service-mindedness. It creates awareness about health, hygiene, first aid and the importance of helping people in need.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

To inspire young volunteers to promote humanity, health and service. The programme encourages students to help others without discrimination and contribute to healthier and safer communities.

WHAT WE AIM FOR

Objectives

  • Promote humanitarian values.
  • Create health and hygiene awareness.
  • Encourage voluntary service.
  • Develop first-aid awareness.
  • Promote compassion and cooperation.
  • Encourage healthy lifestyles.
OUR INITIATIVES

Major Activities

  • Blood donation awareness programmes.
  • Health awareness camps.
  • Personal hygiene programmes.
  • First-aid awareness sessions.
  • Community service activities.
  • Volunteer development programmes.

04 / HEALTH & SOCIAL AWARENESS

Red Ribbon Club (RRC)

"Awareness, Prevention and Compassion"

04
GET TO KNOW US

About RRC

The Red Ribbon Club aims to create awareness among students about HIV and AIDS prevention, health education and responsible behaviour. It encourages young people to become informed volunteers and spread awareness in society.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

To create an informed and health-conscious student community through awareness, education and peer participation. The programme promotes prevention, understanding and a stigma-free society.

WHAT WE AIM FOR

Objectives

  • Create awareness about HIV and AIDS.
  • Promote accurate health information.
  • Encourage responsible behaviour.
  • Reduce stigma and discrimination.
  • Develop peer educators.
  • Promote healthy lifestyles.
OUR INITIATIVES

Major Activities

  • HIV and AIDS awareness sessions.
  • Peer education programmes.
  • Health education campaigns.
  • Awareness rallies and events.
  • Community outreach programmes.
  • Information and education activities.
