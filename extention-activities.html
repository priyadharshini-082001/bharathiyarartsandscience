Student Activities & Services

Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women encourages students to participate in social service, leadership, community development, health awareness and nation-building activities. Through various student organisations, students develop responsibility, confidence, teamwork and a spirit of service.

NSS NCC YRC RRC Student Council
01
SOCIAL SERVICE

National Service Scheme (NSS)

The National Service Scheme at Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women, Deviyakurichi, functions with three units comprising 100 volunteers in each unit and three NSS Programme Officers. Each unit adopts a village and conducts a special camp every year. Volunteers create awareness about social issues, education, cleanliness and community welfare through rallies, stage programmes and other service activities.

Special camp activities include temple cleaning, sapling plantation, health camps and community development initiatives.

03 NSS Units
300 Volunteers
03 Programme Officers

Vision

  • To inculcate spontaneous willingness to savour pleasant and positive thoughts in young minds.
  • To enhance the meaning of life.
  • To strive for righteous human pursuits.
  • To address the intellectual, emotional and educational needs of society.
  • To sensitise society and encourage the younger generation in nation-building.

Mission

  • To provide quality education.
  • To develop total personality through co-curricular and extracurricular activities.
  • To inculcate human values like mutual love, truth and non-violence.
  • To motivate achievement of excellence in every field of life.
  • To extend guidance and consultancy services.
OUR MOTTO

NOT ME BUT YOU

The NSS motto reflects the essence of democratic living and the need for selfless service. It emphasises that individual welfare is ultimately connected with the welfare of society as a whole.

Objectives

  • Understand the community in which volunteers work.
  • Understand themselves in relation to their community.
  • Identify community needs and problems and participate in problem-solving.
  • Develop a sense of social and civic responsibility.
  • Use knowledge to find practical solutions to individual and community problems.
  • Develop competence in group living and sharing responsibilities.
  • Gain skills in mobilising community participation.
  • Acquire leadership qualities and democratic attitudes.
  • Develop the capacity to respond to emergencies and natural disasters and promote national integration and social harmony.

DISCIPLINE & LEADERSHIP

National Cadet Corps (NCC)

The National Cadet Corps is a student development platform that promotes discipline, leadership, unity and a spirit of service. NCC activities encourage students to develop confidence, teamwork, responsibility and a commitment towards the nation.

Cadets can develop personal discipline and leadership abilities through structured training, community participation and outdoor activities.

Unity & Discipline

Building confident, responsible and service-minded citizens.

Vision

To develop disciplined, confident and responsible young citizens with leadership qualities and a commitment to national service.

Mission

To encourage teamwork, character development, physical fitness, discipline and service through meaningful cadet activities.

Objectives

  • Develop discipline, leadership and self-confidence.
  • Promote unity, teamwork and national integration.
  • Encourage physical fitness and responsible citizenship.
  • Develop service-mindedness and community responsibility.
  • Build courage, confidence and decision-making skills.

HEALTH & AWARENESS

Red Ribbon Club (RRC)

The Red Ribbon Club is a youth-focused initiative that promotes awareness about HIV and AIDS, preventive health practices, responsible behaviour and community participation. It encourages students to become informed ambassadors of health awareness and social responsibility.

Awareness Saves Lives

Encouraging knowledge, prevention and responsible choices.

Vision

To create an informed and supportive student community that promotes health awareness, dignity and responsible behaviour.

Mission

To provide awareness and education on HIV prevention, healthy lifestyles and the importance of eliminating stigma and discrimination.

Objectives

  • Increase awareness about HIV and AIDS.
  • Promote accurate health information and prevention awareness.
  • Encourage healthy and responsible lifestyles.
  • Reduce stigma and discrimination through education.
  • Develop student volunteers as peer educators.

SERVICE • DISCIPLINE • LEADERSHIP

Scouts & Guides

The Scouts and Guides movement encourages students to become responsible, disciplined, self-reliant and service-minded citizens. It provides opportunities to develop leadership, teamwork, practical skills and a spirit of helping others. Through various activities, students learn the importance of community service, environmental responsibility, personal development and national integration.

The movement promotes character building through outdoor learning, group activities, service initiatives and participation in community development programmes.

Be Prepared

Building character, courage, discipline and a spirit of service.

Vision

To nurture responsible, confident and compassionate individuals who uphold the values of discipline, integrity, leadership and service to humanity.

Mission

To provide opportunities for students to develop character, practical skills, teamwork and leadership through learning experiences, outdoor activities and community service.

Objectives

  • Develop discipline, self-confidence and leadership qualities.
  • Encourage self-reliance and responsible citizenship.
  • Promote teamwork, cooperation and mutual respect.
  • Develop practical skills and problem-solving abilities.
  • Encourage environmental awareness and conservation.
  • Instil the spirit of service and helping others.
  • Promote national integration and social harmony.
  • Prepare students to respond responsibly during emergencies.
