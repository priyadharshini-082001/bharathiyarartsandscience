CAMPUS CONNECTIVITY

Transportation

Bharathiyar Arts & Science College for Women, Deviyakurichi (Autonomous), provides transportation connectivity for students and staff. The college transport network covers important locations within a 100 km radius in all directions, supporting convenient travel to and from the institution.

SAFE • RELIABLE • CONVENIENT

The institution operates buses covering important points across the surrounding region. The transportation facility supports students and staff with regular connectivity to the college.
